# Business Domains

Business specs: behaviour, data, rules and display per domain. One file per domain; test cases in [test-cases.md](test-cases.md).

| # | Domain | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Baby Progress](business/1-baby-progress.md) | Gestational age, due date and child age worked out from the last period date; dashboard summary and baby picker; guide steps (week 1–40, month 0–24, age 0–18), reference figures with the change since the previous month or year, and guide pages |
| 2 | [Care Guide](business/2-care-guide.md) | Supplements, what to eat and what to avoid per week, month and age |
| 3 | [Vaccination Record](business/3-vaccination.md) | TCMR schedule, per-baby dose records, dose status, dashboard card, schedule page |
| 4 | [Prenatal Tests](business/4-prenatal-tests.md) | Prenatal screening schedule (NIPT), per-baby test records, test status, week page section, dashboard card, schedule page |
| 5 | [Feeding and Sleep](business/5-feeding-sleep.md) | Feed interval, milk per feed, feeds or meals per day and sleep hours per month and age, with their sources |
| 6 | [Skills to Teach](business/6-skills.md) | Skills to teach per month and age, paraphrased from the book *Em phải đến Harvard học kinh tế* |
