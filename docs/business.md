# Business Domains

Business specs: behaviour, data, rules and display per domain. One file per domain; test cases in [test-cases.md](test-cases.md).

| # | Domain | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Baby Progress](business/1-baby-progress.md) | Gestational age, due date and child age worked out from the last period date; dashboard summary and baby picker; guide steps (week 1–40, month 0–24, age 0–18), reference figures and guide pages |
| 2 | [Care Guide](business/2-care-guide.md) | Supplements, what to eat and what to avoid per week, month and age |
| 3 | [Vaccination Record](business/3-vaccination.md) | TCMR schedule, per-baby dose records, dose status, dashboard card, schedule page |
